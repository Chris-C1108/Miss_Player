# Lessons Learned — `v5.7.118-beta.052` (2026-11-16 19:35:00)

## 1. 增量追加分支 (`patchSectionContent`) 必须与全量渲染共享同一过滤与补齐契约
- **问题根因**：`CommentCardRenderer` 全量重绘路径已正确读取 `highlightOnly`，但 `CommentPanel.patchSectionContent(siteKey)` 为了追求增量 DOM 追加性能，直接遍历 `site.filteredComments.filter(c => !existingIdSet.has(c.id))` 而遗漏了 `this.isHighlightFilterActive` 判断，导致用户在高能现场激活态下翻页或异步拉到新站点数据时，普通评论再次直接泄漏入 DOM。
- **反模式禁令**：
  - `FORBIDDEN: Filtering only in full renderCommentsList while letting incremental patchSectionContent append raw filteredComments`
  - `USE: Apply the exact same isHighlightFilterActive predicate inside patchSectionContent, update tab/filter counters, and re-invoke checkViewportFill() when filtered pages yield too few highlight cards.`

## 2. 多源标签卡左右滑动不能只位移当前激活面板
- **问题根因**：旧版横向滑动仅把 `activeSection` 平移出屏外，由于邻近面板仍带有 `.is-hidden-source { display: none !important; }`，右侧暴露出纯黑空白背景，松手后瞬间清零 `transform` 并同步 `setActiveSource()`，视觉上就是“晃动一下生硬闪切”。
- **反模式禁令**：
  - `FORBIDDEN: Translating only the active section over display:none neighbors and snapping synchronously on touchend`
  - `USE: Synchronize adjacent source panels (.is-swipe-adjacent) at ±(stageWidth + gap) with matching min-height: 100%, rounded card edges, and GPU translate3d(), animate both cards in lockstep on release, and defer concurrent renderCommentsList() mutations until the transition settles.`

## 3. 隐藏源与多条件查询选择器必须限定 `.is-active-source`
- **问题根因**：`this.commentsList.querySelector('.tm-comment-section:not(.is-collapsed)')` 在 Jable 被 `.is-hidden-source` 隐藏且 JavDB 为激活源时，依然会在 DOM 顺序上优先命中 Jable，导致纵向滑动触发错误站点的翻页请求。
- **反模式禁令**：
  - `FORBIDDEN: Querying .tm-comment-section:not(.is-collapsed) without .is-active-source in multi-source tabbed panels`
  - `USE: Always scope active-pane queries with .tm-comment-section.is-active-source:not(.is-collapsed), and guard vertical touch handlers while horizontal card swipe/animation is active.`

## 4. 源码字符串切片测试应锚定方法定义而非首个调用点
- **问题根因**：`panelContent.indexOf('patchSectionContent(siteKey)')` 在单测中命中了第 1430 行的调用点 `this.patchSectionContent(siteKey)` 而非第 2560 行的方法体定义，导致 CI 误报。
- **反模式禁令**：
  - `FORBIDDEN: Anchoring source-slice assertions on bare methodName(args) that matches call sites earlier in the file`
  - `USE: Anchor on methodName(args) { (and bound the end slice at the next method signature) when asserting method body contracts.`
